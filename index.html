An account pays a stated annual rate of 8% compounded quarterly. The effective annual rate is closest to:

A8.00%
B8.16%
C8.24%

Explanation EAR = (1 + 0.08/4)^4 − 1 = 1.02^4 − 1 = 8.24%. Semiannual compounding would give 8.16%.

Fixed IncomeL1-FI-52

A bond has a modified duration of 6.0 and an annual convexity of 50. If its yield falls by 75 bps, the estimated percentage price change is closest to:

A+4.36%
B+4.50%
C+4.64%

Explanation%ΔP ≈ −6.0 × (−0.0075) + ½ × 50 × (0.0075)² = 4.50% + 0.14% = +4.64%. The convexity term is positive whether yields rise or fall. 4.50% is the duration estimate alone, and 4.36% subtracts the convexity adjustment.
